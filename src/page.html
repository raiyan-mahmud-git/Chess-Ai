How to use it

Playing

  • Drag a piece, or click it and click its destination. The board only accepts legal moves for the side to move.
  • Right-click drag on the board draws arrows; right-click a square marks it. Left-click removes them.
  • Arrow keys move the keyboard cursor, Enter selects and moves, Esc cancels.
  • ← / → step through the game when the board is not focused.

Engine

Piece style, board palette, theme and coordinates live in the Settings menu (the ☰ button). Search runs in a worker. Depth, score, nodes and the principal variation are live. Hint asks for a move without playing it. Analysis board mode keeps the engine running after every move.
